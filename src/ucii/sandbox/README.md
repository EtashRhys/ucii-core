# UCII Sandbox Research Environment

## Purpose

The UCII sandbox is a research environment for experiencing identity primitives.

It does not create production identities, production credentials, or real cryptographic proofs.

The purpose is to demonstrate the relationship between:

- Identity
- Capabilities
- Proof Relationships
- Cryptographic Credentials


## Core Principle

Identity is the root primitive.

Capabilities, proofs, and credentials attach to an identity, but they do not become the identity itself.


Example:

Identity
|
|-- Capabilities
|
|-- Proof Relationships
|
|-- Credentials


## Sandbox Experiences

### Identity

An identity represents the root object.

Example:

Human Identity
Device Identity
AI Agent Identity
Organization Identity


### Capabilities

Capabilities represent what an identity may participate in or perform.

Examples:

verification
authorization
access


### Proof Relationships

Proofs demonstrate relationships involving an identity.

Examples:

ownership
control
authorization
authenticity


### Credentials

Credentials are cryptographic assertions associated with an identity.

The sandbox does not issue production credentials.


## Research Intent

The sandbox exists to help researchers understand:

- identity is separate from authentication
- identity is separate from credentials
- identity is separate from permissions
- relationships extend identity without replacing it


This layer is intentionally minimal.
